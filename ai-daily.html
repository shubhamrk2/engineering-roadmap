How to use a day

Learn is what to read or watch, kept short so you are typing within the first hour. Do is the exercise or build task. Done when is the pass/fail test: if you cannot meet it, the day is not done, and you move the leftover to Saturday’s catch-up instead of starting new material. The note box is for the specific thing you got stuck on; that sentence is what you review on Saturday.

Red pencil — falling behind

A few open days is normal. If you have more than three open days, do not try to double up: you will learn less and burn out. Drop DSA volume first, then optional reading, then Project 2 scope. Gates and lab tests are never dropped. If a gate fails, the plan resets from that week, not from today.
